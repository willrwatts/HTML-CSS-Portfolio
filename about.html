About Me

Hello! There isn't too much I really want to say because this is a personal website more than a public one. But anyways, I am a graphic designer with a passion for photography. The old and analogue fascinate me and greatly influence my photographic work. My main work focuses on space and feeling in these spaces. (However, this website does not display all of my work and more so is a holding space for the unpolished extra pieces I have lying around.) Being from Nashville, most of my work is from Tennessee and influenced by it in some way, shape, or form. The bottom line is that I like photography.


Since this website isn't official, I can really say whatever I want. It's really awkward putting more than one photo of yourself on a page. Not only do I have to stare myself in the eyes while I code, but now I have to look two of me in the eyes. It's such a weird experience. Not to mention that by coding them, I control them and I feel really bad.
