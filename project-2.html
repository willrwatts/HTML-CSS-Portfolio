Self Photos

These photos were initially taken for my darkroom class and have kinda stuck with me because they are the only decent photos I have taken of myself. The downside of this is that all of these pictures are of me being strange and doing weird things. I believe the theme of the project was somewhat about "providing proof of self without the body being present." The majority of the meaning has been lost to my poor memory but I think what remains is still interesting.
