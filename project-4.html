Spaces

Spaces is kind of a strange mini project that started out with me taking photos of uninteresting areas in rooms. It is a hobby I have had for a few years and I am not too sure when it started. However, I remember noticing that many of my photos were of strange corners, walls, and floors of buildings that didn't have any immediate appeal. Many of these photos are scattered throughout various film rolls and SD cards so this isn't the full extent of the selection by any means.
